[Nota: UFW no instalado; filtrado y NAT gestionados nativamente por iptables para evitar colisión con Docker]

$ sudo iptables -S FORWARD
-P FORWARD ACCEPT
-A FORWARD -j DOCKER-USER
-A FORWARD -j DOCKER-FORWARD
-A FORWARD -i enp0s3 -o enp0s8 -m conntrack --ctstate RELATED,ESTABLISHED -j ACCEPT
-A FORWARD -i enp0s8 -o enp0s3 -j ACCEPT

$ sudo iptables -S DOCKER-USER
-N DOCKER-USER
-A DOCKER-USER -i enp0s8 -o enp0s3 -j ACCEPT
-A DOCKER-USER -i enp0s3 -o enp0s8 -m conntrack --ctstate RELATED,ESTABLISHED -j ACCEPT

[PASS] Cadena DOCKER-USER preserva tráfico LAN enp0s8 y salida WAN enp0s3. Reglas persistentes en /etc/iptables/rules.v4.
